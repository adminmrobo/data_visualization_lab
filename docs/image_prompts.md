## 1. 표지 (16:9)

16-bit RPG pixel art, a bright classroom inside a research lab with a clean white-and-pale-gray background. A large blackboard-style screen on the back wall shows a 3x3 grid of simple chart pictograms drawn only with lines and bars (a bell-shaped histogram, a rising curve, a dotted scatter, a 2x2 colored square, a U-shaped curve, a bar chart, a dendrogram tree, a wave line) — no letters or numbers. At the front, chibi scientist teacher (thin teal rectangular glasses, spiky black hair with one white streak, small ponytail, white lab coat over black vest and tie) pointing at the screen with a pointer stick. Two teaching assistants beside him: a boxy beige retro CRT-monitor robot with mint pixel face, abacus-bead antenna and tank treads, holding a wooden abacus; and an anthropomorphic fruit fly researcher (black body, big red compound eyes, blue helmet with gold stripe and pink and blue cables, white lab coat over black vest, black gloves) hovering with a magnifying glass. In the student desks facing the screen: a girl (long pink hair with two round buns and a navy streak, blue eyes, white lab coat over lavender hoodie, jeans) raising her hand eagerly; two maid interns, one with long straight black hair holding a small signal flag, one with ash-beige wavy hair and white frilled headband with a pink teacup and notebook; and three small mysterious students in dark hoodies with shadowed faces sitting in the back row, one raising a hand with a question-mark speech bubble icon. Soft daylight, cheerful study mood. No text, no logos. 16:9.
