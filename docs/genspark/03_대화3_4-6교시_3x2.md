## 공통 지시 (맨 처음 한 번 붙여 넣기)

Same characters and same 16-bit RPG pixel art style as the attached cover image and reference sheets. Bright classroom in a research lab, white and pale gray background. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos. Charts are simple lines, bars and dots only. Aspect ratio 3:2 for every image in this conversation.
Roles: scientist = teacher, CRT robot and fruit fly = teaching assistants, pink-haired girl + two maids + three silhouette students (X1 ponytail/pink bow, X2 beret/teal glasses, X3 cat-ear hood/yellow pom-poms) = students.

## 1번 · 4교시 진단과 해석 (저장: scene_g4.png)

3:2. On the board: two curves — one keeps going down, the other goes down then bends back up (a U shape). The robot turns a big dial labeled only with an up arrow to maximum, sweating; the fly assistant points at the point where the U-curve starts rising and holds up a small stop sign (no text on it, just a red octagon). Students watch; X2 adjusts its teal glasses. No text.

The robot tries to split one bowl into two; the fly assistant shakes its head kindly. The teacher holds a small tree diagram (dendrogram) on a clipboard. No text.

## 3번 · 6교시 시계열 (저장: scene_g6.png)

3:2. A long wavy line runs across a wide board like a paper tape, with a smooth thick line through the middle of the waves. A wall calendar shows repeating snowflake icons every twelfth page (no numbers). The light-brown-haired maid points to the calendar; the teacher nods. The robot looks at only the last wave peak, excited. No text.

The fly assistant flies above like a tour guide, pointing at two connected cards. The pink-haired girl and the two maids look up from below. No text.
